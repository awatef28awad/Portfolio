REC-01 · road-safety Public Safety

Road Accident Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard analyzing three years of UK road casualty data, breaking severity, vehicle type, road conditions and time trends down into a single filterable view.

ToolPower BI
Records417,883 casualties
Timeframe2021 – 2023
BreakdownsVehicle, road type, surface, light
Insight — Single carriageways accounted for the largest share of casualties (309.7K), and wet road surfaces stood out as a major severity driver worth flagging to road maintenance planners.

REC-02 · people-analytics Human Resources

HR Analytics Dashboard

An attrition-focused workforce dashboard, segmenting 1,470 employee records by department, age band, education field and job satisfaction to find where the company was actually losing people.

ToolPower BI
Records1,470 employees
Attrition rate16.12% (237 employees)
BreakdownsDept, age, gender, education
Insight — Under-25 and 25–34 age bands showed the sharpest attrition (52–62%), pointing to early-career retention — not senior turnover — as the real risk area.
